# Public three-paint measurement coverage audit

Declared 2026-10-01 following the single attenuation revision. The user approved
examining additional public three-pigment measurements before another revision.
This is source research and coverage preparation, not model fitting or scoring.

Inspect full recipe tables in the cached Asadi/Old Holland and Grillini releases.
Count no-white ternaries, paint combinations, ratio diversity, pure/tint/binary
calibration support, and overlap with the previously selected four-paint studies.
Count paint ingredients as supplied by each source; a commercial tube paint can
itself contain more than one pigment. Do not pool spectra across different binders,
paint identities, preparations or measurement geometries.

Check other public leads against numeric spectra, recorded recipes and physical
sample identity where available. Distinguish measured samples from synthetic
spectra, repeated acquisitions, white-containing mixtures, and derivative copies
of existing data. Published sample counts alone do not establish a new benchmark.
Use only publicly accessible files; do not contact authors or download large raw
image cubes when small tables/documentation suffice.

Preserve hashes and reproducible coverage counts. Prepare an unscored next-study
cohort using all no-white Old Holland ternaries outside the existing columns
1/3/5/8 subset, with all pure/binary samples inside each triple-plus-white palette
as the calibration pool. Selection depends on recipe coverage, not model errors.
Record every selected source row and calibration row, including sparse binary
support. Keep the previous three Old Holland ternaries separate as known controls.
Do not fit a new parameter, evaluate new accuracy, or change the runtime in this
audit. State that a same-source extension is not an independent external dataset.
